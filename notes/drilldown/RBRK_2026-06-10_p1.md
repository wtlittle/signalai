        <div class="subsection-title">Where RBRK sits in the stack</div>
        <p>Rubrik operates at the intersection of data protection and cybersecurity, delivering an enterprise-grade, SaaS-delivered data security platform that spans backup, recovery, ransomware resilience, and data risk governance across hybrid and multicloud estates. Its customer value proposition is anchored on guaranteeing recoverability and compliance of mission-critical data rather than just storing backup copies, which gives RBRK an entry point into both infrastructure and security budgets. The business is best thought of as an ARR-led, infrastructure-adjacent security platform, where multi-year subscriptions, expanding workloads, and seat-based modules drive durable contracted growth. As cloud adoption matures and ransomware attacks remain frequent, Rubrik’s positioning as a “last line of defense” is resonating, supporting elevated growth even at a mid-teens billion-dollar equity value.</p>
        <div class="subsection-title">Core financial setup</div>
        <p>On the numbers, Rubrik is growing far faster than the broader infrastructure software cohort, with LTM revenue of <a href="claim:6">$1.42B</a> growing <span class="pos"><a href="claim:7">39.0%</a></span>, versus cross-sector comps like GLBE at 32.8%, GNRC at 12.4%, and CF at 19.4%. EV/Revenue at <a href="claim:4">9.94x</a> screens rich versus those comps at 2–5x, but the spread is partially justified by an 80.6% gross margin<a href="claim:8"></a> and a 30.9% FCF margin<a href="claim:10"></a> that already rival more mature software franchises. Rubrik remains loss-making on a GAAP operating basis with a -13.6% operating margin<a href="claim:9"></a>, but the combination of rapid top-line expansion and strong cash conversion signals a model that is already economically sound once non-cash and go-to-market investments are normalized. Consensus embeds a deceleration to 24.9% FY1 revenue growth on <a href="claim:24">$1.64B</a> and 21.7% in FY2 on <a href="claim:27">$2.00B</a>, with EPS inflecting from losses to <a href="claim:26">$0.31</a> and <a href="claim:29">$0.65</a> respectively.</p>
        <div class="subsection-title">Thesis frame and swing variables</div>
        <p>The debate is whether Rubrik can compound ARR and revenue above 25% while sustaining 25–30%+ FCF margins as scale kicks in, thereby supporting a double-digit EV/Revenue multiple through the next 1–3 years. Bulls argue that ransomware-driven urgency and the shift from legacy backup to integrated data security platforms will keep expansion motion robust, supporting durable high-teens to low-20s net new ARR contribution per year. Bears focus on intensifying competition from hyperscalers and incumbent backup vendors, as well as on the possibility that growth normalizes toward low-20s quickly while Rubrik is still carrying elevated SBC and go-to-market spend, which would compress valuation toward 6–8x EV/Revenue. At the current share price of <a href="claim:1">$71.74</a>, the market is implicitly underwriting a path where Rubrik steps into the role of a category consolidator and proves that its security capabilities are more than a marketing label, translating into expanding wallet share per customer.</p>
        <div class="subsection-title">Risk-reward snapshot</div>
        <p>The upside case leans on continued beat-and-raise quarters driven by ARR and RPO strength, combined with incremental disclosure that evidences durable security-led expansions rather than one-off migrations. In that scenario, a re-rating to 12–13x NTM EV/Revenue on mid-20s growth can plausibly push the stock toward the high double-digits to low triple-digits, in line with the sell-side target range of <a href="claim:16">$126</a> high and <a href="claim:17">$85</a> low. The downside case sees a derate back toward infrastructure software peers if growth slows more quickly than expected or if FCF conversion compresses, putting a 6–7x multiple and a share price in the $40s back in play as the market treats Rubrik as a standard backup vendor. With consensus skewed bullish (<a href="claim:18">strong buy</a> from 28 analysts<a href="claim:19"></a>) and a still-short public history, positioning risk ahead of each print is meaningful, but the setup remains asymmetric for investors who believe in Rubrik’s ability to monetize its security platform narrative.</p>
