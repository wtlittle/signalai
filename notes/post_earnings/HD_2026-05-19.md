# Home Depot (HD) — Post-Earnings Note
**Reported:** 2026-05-19 | **Day Post:** 6
**Generated:** 2026-05-25 21:55

---

## Headline

Home Depot delivered a modest Q1 FY26 top-line beat with in-line execution, but EPS declined year over year as margin pressure from the GMS acquisition and mix offset steady demand.

**Beat/Miss Quality:** Beat on revenue and adjusted EPS, but only modestly; quality was mixed-to-good because the beat came from steady comp sales and solid ticket growth, not one-time noise.

## Key Metrics

- Net sales: $41.765B vs. $41.59B Street; +4.8% YoY ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf))
- Adjusted diluted EPS: $3.43 vs. $3.41 Street; -3.7% YoY ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf))
- Reported diluted EPS: $3.30 vs. $3.45 last year; -4.3% YoY ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf))
- Comparable sales: +0.6% vs. -0.3% in Q1 FY25; U.S. comp sales +0.4% ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf))
- Gross margin: 33.0% vs. 33.75% implied by ~75 bp decline YoY; management said the decline reflected mix from the GMS acquisition ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf))
- Operating margin: 11.9% vs. 12.9% last year; adjusted operating margin 12.3% vs. 13.2% ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf))
- Customer transactions: 391.1M vs. 394.8M last year; -0.9% YoY, while average ticket rose to $92.76 from $90.71 (+2.3%) ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf))

## Guidance and Tone

FY26 guidance was reaffirmed: total sales growth ~2.5% to 4.5%, comp sales flat to +2.0%, diluted EPS and adjusted EPS both up approximately flat to +4% vs. FY25, with gross margin ~33.1%, operating margin 12.4% to 12.6% and adjusted operating margin 12.8% to 13.0% ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf), [Home Depot IR transcript](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf)). Street consensus for the next quarter was not clearly disclosed in the sources gathered, so I’m leaving next-Q vs. Street as not disclosed.

**Management Tone:** Neutral-to-bullish. Management repeatedly said results were “in line with our expectations” and emphasized resilience in the core customer, with Ted Decker adding that “the underlying demand in our business was relatively similar to what we saw throughout fiscal 2025, despite greater consumer uncertainty and housing affordability pressure” ([Home Depot IR transcript](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf)).

## Surprises / Disappointments

- Positive: Revenue and adjusted EPS both came in slightly ahead of Street, while comp sales stayed positive despite weak housing activity ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf)).
- Positive: Gross margin and opex were described as in line with expectations, suggesting no major execution miss despite integration costs ([Home Depot IR transcript](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf)).
- Negative: Reported diluted EPS declined 4.3% YoY and operating margin compressed 100 bps, showing earnings power is still under pressure ([Home Depot IR](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/press-release/q1-2026-earnings-release.pdf), [Home Depot IR transcript](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf)).
- Negative: April comps were negative (total -0.5%, U.S. -0.8%), indicating some late-quarter softness ([Home Depot IR transcript](https://ir.homedepot.com/~/media/Files/H/HomeDepot-IR/2026/hd-1q26-transcript.pdf)).
