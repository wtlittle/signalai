# NVIDIA (NVDA) — Post-Earnings Note
**Reported:** 2026-05-20 | **Day Post:** 5
**Generated:** 2026-05-25 21:55

---

## Headline

NVIDIA delivered another blockbuster quarter, with Q1 FY27 revenue up 85% year over year and Q2 guidance well above Street expectations, but the stock reaction was muted as investors focused on China risk and lofty expectations.

**Beat/Miss Quality:** Beat on revenue, EPS, margins, and guidance; quality was strong, not noise, because the beat was driven by broad-based AI/data center demand and strong gross margins rather than one-time items.

- Non-GAAP EPS: $1.87 vs $1.76 Street est.; +130.9% YoY from $0.81 ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx), [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/nvidia-nasdaqnvda-announces-quarterly-earnings-results-2026-05-20/))
- GAAP EPS: $2.39, with GAAP gross margin 74.9% and non-GAAP gross margin 75.0% ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx))

- Free cash flow: $48.6B, up from $26.1B a year ago ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/were-raising-our-price-target-on-nvidia-after-another-knockout-quarter-and-guide-.html))
- Shareholder returns: board authorized an additional $80.0B buyback and raised the quarterly dividend to $0.25/share from $0.01 ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/were-raising-our-price-target-on-nvidia-after-another-knockout-quarter-and-guide-.html))

## Guidance and Tone

Q2 FY27 revenue guide was $91.0B (±2%), versus Street consensus around $86.84B to $86.9B; Q2 adjusted gross margin guide was ~75.0% (±50 bps) vs ~74.5% Street, adjusted opex was guided to $8.3B, and management said it expects zero data center compute revenue from China in the outlook ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/were-raising-our-price-target-on-nvidia-after-another-knockout-quarter-and-guide-.html), [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/NVDA/earnings/)).

**Management Tone:** Bullish. Jensen Huang said, “The buildout of AI factories — the largest infrastructure expansion in human history — is accelerating at extraordinary speed,” and added, “Agentic AI has arrived, doing productive work, generating real value and scaling rapidly across companies and industries” ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).

## Surprises / Disappointments

- Positive: record revenue of $81.62B and Q2 revenue guide of $91B both cleared consensus by a wide margin ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/were-raising-our-price-target-on-nvidia-after-another-knockout-quarter-and-guide-.html)).
- Positive: gross margin held at 75% despite the scale of growth, showing pricing power and mix strength ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).
- Positive: the board authorized an $80B buyback and lifted the dividend to $0.25/share, signaling confidence in cash generation ([NVIDIA IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).
- Negative: management again excluded China data center compute revenue from the guide, leaving geopolitics a live overhang ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/were-raising-our-price-target-on-nvidia-after-another-knockout-quarter-and-guide-.html)).
- Negative: the stock response was muted/slightly down even after a major beat, implying expectations were already very high ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/21/here-we-go-again-with-nvidia-falling-on-earnings-what-the-sellers-are-missing.html)).
