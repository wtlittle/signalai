## Headline

Zoom beat Q1 FY27 expectations with stronger enterprise growth, expanding margins, and an increased buyback authorization, then raised full-year guidance on the back of AI momentum and resilient cash generation.

**Beat/Miss Quality:** Beat, and it looked like quality rather than noise: revenue, EPS, enterprise growth, margins, cash flow, and guidance all moved in the right direction, while the only softness was in lower-growth Online and a modest churn uptick.

## Guidance and Tone

FY27 revenue guided to $5.080B-$5.090B vs Street around $5.08B; FY27 non-GAAP EPS guided to $5.96-$6.00 vs Street around $5.49 (per MarketBeat consensus summaries), with FY27 free cash flow guided to $1.700B-$1.740B ([Zoom IR](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-communications-reports-financial-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027/), [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/zoom-communications-nasdaqzm-releases-fy-2027-earnings-guidance-2026-05-21/)).

**Management Tone:** Bullish. Eric Yuan said, “We saw continued momentum in the first quarter…exceeding the high end of our guidance,” and framed Zoom as an “AI-first system of action” with AI Companion paid users up 184% YoY ([Zoom IR](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-communications-reports-financial-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027/)).

## Surprises / Disappointments

- Positive: EPS and revenue both beat, while enterprise revenue grew faster than overall revenue ([Zoom IR](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-communications-reports-financial-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027/)).
- Positive: Management raised full-year revenue/EPS outlook and added $1.0B to the share repurchase authorization ([Zoom IR](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-communications-reports-financial-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027/)).
- Positive: AI Companion paid users grew 184% YoY and My Notes reached 1.5M licensed users in four months ([Zoom IR](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-communications-reports-financial-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027/)).
- Negative: Online revenue growth remained only 2.8% YoY, and online monthly churn ticked up to 3.0% from 2.8% ([Zoom IR](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-communications-reports-financial-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027/)).
