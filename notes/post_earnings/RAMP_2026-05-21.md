## Headline

LiveRamp beat Q4 FY26 estimates on revenue and non-GAAP EPS, but the print was overshadowed by the announced all-cash acquisition by Publicis Groupe.

**Beat/Miss Quality:** Beat on revenue and non-GAAP EPS; quality was solid, with broad-based operating leverage, improving ARR/CRPO, and strong cash generation rather than one-off noise.

## Guidance and Tone

No next-quarter or full-year revenue/EPS guidance was provided; LiveRamp said it would not host the scheduled earnings call or provide financial guidance because of the announced transaction with Publicis Groupe ([LiveRamp IR](https://investors.liveramp.com/node/24026/pdf)).

**Management Tone:** Bullish/constructive on the business, though the call was effectively muted by the deal announcement; CEO Scott Howe said, “We finished FY26 on a strong note, with Q4 revenue and operating income ahead of consensus and ARR growth accelerating sequentially” ([LiveRamp IR](https://investors.liveramp.com/node/24026/pdf)).
