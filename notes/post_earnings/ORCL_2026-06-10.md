**FY2027 outlook (pre-announced March 2026):** Oracle raised FY2027 revenue guidance to ~$90B at the Q3 call, implying ~34% YoY growth from the ~$67B FY2026 target. Management stated that demand for cloud and AI continues to outpace supply.

**Q4 FY2026 company guidance (issued March 2026):** Revenue +19-21% in USD; total cloud revenue +46-50% in USD; non-GAAP EPS $1.96-$2.00.

**Management Tone (going into print):** Highly constructive. Safra Catz stated "OCI revenue growth is expected to be dramatically higher than our competitors — hyper-growth is continuing." Significant capex commitment ($50B FY2026, $45-50B planned fundraise) with management noting that >90% of new data center capacity is funded by partner capital or customer prepayments. Long-term roadmap: $144B in cloud infrastructure revenue by FY2030, described as "already booked in our reported RPO."

**Post-print guidance:** FY2027 reaffirmation of $90B target and any update to capex / FCF inflection timeline will be the primary market-moving guidance item.

## Segments

**Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)**

OCI is the primary growth driver. Q3 FQ2026 OCI revenue was $4.9B (+84% YoY). Consensus for Q4 targets ~$5.17B (+~72% YoY). OCI includes AI GPU clusters for OpenAI, Meta, xAI; demand described as exceeding supply capacity as of Q3. **Cloud Applications (SaaS)**

Q3 cloud apps revenue was ~$4.0B (+13% YoY). Q4 consensus targets ~$4.16B. Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP, NetSuite, and Healthcare (Oracle Health) are the primary products. Strategic SaaS annualized revenue run-rate hit ~$9.3B (+20% YoY) as of FQ4 2025.

**License and Software Support**

Traditional license revenue declining. Software license support remains a cash-flow contributor. Company is intentionally shifting mix toward cloud.

## Thesis Impact

Oracle's AI infrastructure thesis rests on three pillars: (1) OCI as a credible third hyperscaler for AI workloads, (2) $553B+ RPO as a revenue pipeline stretching into the 2030s, and (3) margin expansion as the capex cycle normalizes. The Q4 print tests whether RPO continues converting to recognized revenue at an accelerating pace. A beat on OCI growth + RPO expansion + firm FY2027 $90B guidance would reinforce the thesis; any softness on capex commentary or guidance hedge would inject uncertainty into the high-valuation AI infrastructure story.
