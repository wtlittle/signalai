# Intuit (INTU) — Post-Earnings Note
**Reported:** 2026-05-20 | **Day Post:** 5
**Generated:** 2026-05-25 21:55

---

## Headline

Intuit delivered a modest revenue miss but an EPS beat, raised full-year guidance, and paired the print with a 17% workforce reduction tied to an AI-driven operating reset.

**Beat/Miss Quality:** Mixed: revenue missed slightly while non-GAAP EPS beat; quality was mixed-to-noisy because upside came alongside cost actions and the revenue shortfall was small, but the core print still showed solid underlying growth.

## Guidance and Tone

FY26: revenue raised to $21.341B-$21.374B versus prior guide of roughly $21.0B-$21.19B; Street was about $21.2B, so the new guide was above consensus ([Intuit IR press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/intuit-intu-q3-earnings-report-2026-company-cutting-17percent-of-staff.html)). FY26 non-GAAP EPS guided to $23.80-$23.85 versus Street at $23.21, also above consensus ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/intuit-intu-q3-earnings-report-2026-company-cutting-17percent-of-staff.html)). Q4 FY26 revenue growth is guided to 11%-12%; non-GAAP EPS is guided to $3.56-$3.62 ([Intuit IR press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance)).

**Management Tone:** Bullish: management emphasized AI-driven execution and growth engines, saying, "We delivered strong third-quarter results, driven by our AI-driven expert platform strategy" and that they were "raising our full-year revenue guidance" ([Intuit IR press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance)).

## Surprises / Disappointments

- Positive: FY26 revenue and non-GAAP EPS guidance were both raised above Street estimates ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/intuit-intu-q3-earnings-report-2026-company-cutting-17percent-of-staff.html)).
- Positive: Global Business Solutions and Online Ecosystem growth stayed strong at 15% and 19% respectively, with the businesses excluding Mailchimp growing even faster ([Intuit IR press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance)).
- Negative: Revenue missed consensus modestly, and TurboTax growth slowed to 7% amid pressure in the most price-sensitive DIY filer cohort ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/intuit-intu-q3-earnings-report-2026-company-cutting-17percent-of-staff.html), [Intuit IR press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance)).
- Negative: Intuit announced a 17% workforce reduction and estimated $300M-$340M of restructuring charges, which weighed on sentiment ([Intuit IR press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance)).

## Thesis Impact

Neutral to mildly reinforcing: the quarter confirmed durable growth in the AI-enabled platforms and a stronger FY26 outlook, but the revenue miss and TurboTax softness keep the near-term debate centered on mix and execution rather than a clean re-rating.
