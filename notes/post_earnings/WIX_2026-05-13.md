# Wix (WIX) — Post-Earnings Note
**Reported:** 2026-05-13 | **Day Post:** 12
**Generated:** 2026-05-25 21:55

---

## Headline

Wix delivered 14% revenue growth and 15% bookings growth in Q1, but a sharp non-GAAP EPS miss and heavier AI/marketing spend triggered a steep selloff despite reiterated full-year guidance.

**Beat/Miss Quality:** Mixed miss: revenue was roughly in line/slightly below Street, while non-GAAP EPS missed materially; the miss looked quality-driven because management tied margin pressure to deliberate AI, Base44, and marketing investments rather than demand deterioration.

## Guidance and Tone

Wix reiterated full-year 2026 guidance for bookings and revenue to grow at a mid-teens percentage rate YoY, and guided Q2 2026 revenue to also grow at a mid-teens percentage rate; the company also expects FY26 free cash flow margin excluding acquisition-related expenses to be in the high teens, or low-to-mid-20% on a pre-tender capital structure basis ([MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-13-wixcom-ltd-stock/); [Wix press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/13/3293644/0/en/wix-reports-first-quarter-2026-results.html))

**Management Tone:** Bullish on AI/platform strategy but cautious on near-term margins; key themes were proprietary LLMs, Harmony, and Base44 as long-term moats, with Nir Zohar saying the company has a “major competitive advantage” and that its own model creates a “flywheel” and “significant competitive advantage” ([MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-13-wixcom-ltd-stock/); [Wix press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/13/3293644/0/en/wix-reports-first-quarter-2026-results.html))

## Surprises / Disappointments

- Positive: bookings outpaced revenue at +15% YoY and Base44 ARR reached $150M by mid-May ([Wix press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/13/3293644/0/en/wix-reports-first-quarter-2026-results.html); [MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-13-wixcom-ltd-stock/))
- Positive: Wix announced its first proprietary LLM powering Harmony, with management saying inference costs should stay low and control improve ([MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-13-wixcom-ltd-stock/))
- Negative: non-GAAP EPS missed Street by a wide margin and gross margin declined due to elevated Base44/AI and marketing spend ([MarketBeat earnings summary](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/scotiabank-issues-pessimistic-forecast-for-wixcom-nasdaqwix-stock-price-2026-05-14/); [MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-13-wixcom-ltd-stock/))
- Negative: Partners growth slowed more than expected because Wix pulled back partner marketing and product timelines slipped due to Israel-related productivity disruption ([MarketBeat transcript](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-13-wixcom-ltd-stock/))

## Thesis Impact

Neutral to slightly challenged: the long-term AI/platform thesis was reinforced, but near-term profitability and execution timing looked worse than expected, prompting a sharp multiple reset.
